# Store Listing Images

Place the following images in this directory before submitting to Google Play or F-Droid:

| File | Dimensions | Format | Required By |
|---|---|---|---|
| `icon.png` | 512×512 px | PNG (32-bit) | Google Play, F-Droid |
| `featureGraphic.png` | 1024×500 px | PNG or JPEG | Google Play |

Place screenshots in `phoneScreenshots/`:
- At least 2 screenshots (F-Droid minimum)
- Google Play recommends 2-8 screenshots
- 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio, PNG or JPEG

Do NOT place actual screenshot files here until the app has a UI to show.
Use `.gitkeep` to preserve the directory structure until then.
