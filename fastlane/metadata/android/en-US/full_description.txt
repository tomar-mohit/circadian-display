Circadian Display continuously adjusts your screen's warmth and brightness throughout the evening based on customizable curves you define. Unlike standard night light solutions with fixed on/off schedules, Circadian Display provides a smooth, gradual transition tailored to your preferences.

Features:
- Dynamic comfort curves with precise warmth and dimming levels over time
- Dual-engine modes: Native (system-level) and Overlay (software-based, works on all devices)
- Background scheduler for automatic, hands-free operation
- Privacy-first: completely offline, tracker-free, no data collection
- Open source (Apache 2.0)
